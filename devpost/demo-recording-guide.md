# Island Rush — screen-recording guide

Record the final game at **https://island-rush-six.vercel.app** and pair it with
[the voice-over script](demo-voiceover.md). Target 2:40–2:50; export below 3:00.
Show the working app from the first second. Current build/deployment evidence
is recorded in [shipping.md](shipping.md), rather than assumed by this guide.

## Prepare real game states

1. Keep your usual island save safe. Use a separate browser profile for a fresh
   start, and another for a later settlement. Do not clear your main save.
2. Capture the game window at 1920×1080 and 30 fps or better, zoom 100%, with
   notifications hidden. Wait for loading to finish. Record narration separately.
3. Learn Left/Right or A/D, Space/Up/W, Pause and **Head home**. Touch supports
   swipes and the visible buttons. Use **Head home** to keep a useful take.
4. Prepare enough timber for a Pioneer Cottage (30), then water (20 timber,
   10 stone), bakery (35/15) and lumber production (20/5). Gather through real
   runs and coin exchanges. The starting two pioneers can work without homes;
   housing brings additional workers. Prioritize water and food before many homes.
5. Build a Town Hall (45/25) to open tier 2. For optional later clips, prepare
   clinic, lodge, pier and lighthouse; a staffed production chain and a stock of
   fish/planks make the trading screen meaningful. Town Hall upgrade costs
   100 timber/80 stone; geothermal requires tier 3 and a spring space.
6. One Outpost (25/10) unlocks four more spaces on its islet, up to 32 total.
   A Bridge Link (65/40, tier 2) connects a fresh islet. Prepare one beforehand
   for the expansion shot, without spending recording time on repeated runs.
7. Let the settlement run normally to record arrivals, health/jobs, production,
   festivals and visitors. Simulation ticks every ten seconds. Lodges refresh
   merchants every two minutes; a safe pier has scheduled 30-second trade
   windows. Do not promise a particular event will happen during one short take.
8. Record later states in advance through normal play. Caption time jumps
   “Later in the same settlement”. Do not inject inventory or fake consequences.
   If an optional ship/visitor clip is unavailable, show its real management
   screen and explain the feature without claiming an event occurred on screen.

## Capture and edit

Record clips separately: island/catalog → run/return → construction/move →
people/supplies → harbor/council/expansion → mobile/repo → closing island.
Keep repeated preparation runs out of the final cut. Label later stages,
accelerated footage and any narrow desktop view as “Mobile layout preview”.
A layout preview is not evidence of physical-phone touch testing.

Use real screenshots/gameplay, not generated concept art as working footage.
The thumbnail may remain separately labelled promotional artwork. Avoid an
introductory slide deck; show the game immediately, including before one minute.
Use English captions if possible. Keep narration clear and use only audio/assets
you have rights to. Do not expose private learner notes, credentials, personal
notifications or account/billing pages while recording the repo.

Review the final export once with sound and once muted. Check the complete
run → bank → build sequence is visible, the UI text is readable, the build
percentage/reveal is not cut out, and the duration is below three minutes.
