## Description — copy from here

Run. Return. Build. Thrive.

Island Rush combines the action of an endless coastal runner with the creativity of growing a living island settlement. Every supply run brings back timber, stone and coins that become homes, services and connections to new islets.

🎮 Play the game: https://island-rush-six.vercel.app/
💻 Source code: https://github.com/simiyu-samuel/islandrush
🏝️ Devpost project: https://devpost.com/software/island-rush

In this demo:
• Run through a coastal jungle, change lanes and jump obstacles as the pace increases.
• Return whenever you choose, or keep collected supplies when a collision ends the run.
• Choose from 16 building types across four categories, watch construction take shape, and move buildings between suitable spaces.
• Support individual islanders with homes, jobs, food, water and healthcare.
• Develop production chains, welcome tourists and merchants, and trade with scheduled cargo ships.
• Unlock policies and advanced buildings, claim more ground and connect further islets.

Created by Simiyu Samuel for Build With AI: Basics using Codex and the Devpost Learn Skill Pack. I directed the concept, visual identity and playtest revisions; AI assisted with implementation and original artwork.

Built with Phaser, TypeScript, Vite, HTML and CSS. Hosted on Vercel. The source is MIT licensed, and the game opens without an account.
